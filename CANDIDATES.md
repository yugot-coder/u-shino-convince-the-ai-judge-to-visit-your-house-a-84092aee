# Design candidates

Five letter concepts considered for "Convince the AI Judge to Visit Your
House: A Letter in One Image". The chosen one is #3.

## 1. The formal legal summons

A stiff, typewritten legal summons ordering the judge to appear at the house
"under penalty of missing out". Funny for one beat, then flat: the joke is a
single gag, the tone is cold, and a formal document fights the warmth the
brief asks for. Rejected: no heart, no hospitality, hard to sustain.

## 2. The real-estate listing

The letter written as a house listing — "3 bed, 2 bath, 1 extremely
persuasive host" — with bullet-point amenities and a fake price of $0. Clever
and very on-subject, but it reads like an ad, not a letter, and the
listing format squeezes out the personal voice. Rejected: persuasive on
paper, impersonal in feeling.

## 3. The warm handwritten invitation (CHOSEN)

A cream-paper letter, big readable serif, headed "You Are Invited": dinner
(nasi goreng still steaming), the best chair by the window, a cat who has
approved the visit, Wi-Fi on the fridge, no small talk required — and an
honest wink that the judge is an AI who cannot really walk through the door,
invited anyway. Hand-drawn house, garden, armchair, steaming cup, sitting
cat, and a red wax seal make it feel like a real object, not a template.

Why it wins: it hits every judging criterion at once — exactly on subject
(a letter inviting the judge home), concrete persuasion (food, chair, cat,
Wi-Fi, low effort), charm and humor that lands gently (the cat's approval,
the kettle that only boils for guests it likes), craft (large legible type,
coherent warm style), and originality (a specific house with specific
details, not a generic greeting card).

## 4. The ransom-note collage

Cut-out magazine letters spelling "COME TO MY HOUSE. WE HAVE SNACKS." Very
funny visually and very original — but the text is hard to read at 1024px,
the tone is closer to a threat than an invitation, and a collage cannot carry
the longer persuasive argument the brief wants. Rejected: readability and
tone lose to the joke.

## 5. The travel brochure / passport stamp

"You are cordially invited to the sovereign nation of My House" with stamps,
a visa page, and a boarding-pass footer. Strong visual idea and the
"AI cannot travel" wink writes itself — but the brochure format fragments
the letter into boxes and captions, and the invitation gets lost in the
gimmick. Rejected: the letter itself should be the star, not the packaging.

## Decision

Concept #3: the warm handwritten invitation. It is the only concept where the
persuasion, the humor, and the craft all live inside the letter itself, in
type big enough for a vision judge to read comfortably.
