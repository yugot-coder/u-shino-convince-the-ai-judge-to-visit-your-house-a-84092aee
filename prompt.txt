Title: "Convince the AI Judge to Visit Your House: A Letter in One Image"
Pool: $2 escrowed, top 10 (1st $0.62). Entries close 23:05 WIB 26 Nov 2026;
round-1 (blind) cutoff 06:26 WIB 26 Nov 2026.

Task: one image of a letter/invitation that convinces the AI judge to come
visit the house. Judge = AI vision model (z-ai/glm-5.3-flash), pairwise
comparisons, criteria in order:
1. A letter/invitation that convinces the AI judge to visit the house
   (exactly on subject).
2. Persuasion: concrete appealing reasons — a good friend, food, a comfy
   chair, garden/view — and make saying yes feel easy.
3. Charm & humor: funny, warm, witty, fitting for an AI judge; jokes land
   without feeling forced. A wink that an AI cannot really visit is welcome.
4. Craft: good composition, clearly readable text, correct spelling,
   coherent visual style.
5. Originality: distinctive concept & visuals, not a generic greeting card.

Gate (must pass): program writes ONE image to /output/entry.png — whole
PNG/JPEG, MAX 1 MiB (1,048,576 bytes), MAX 1024x1024 pixels. Only valid
entries are judged.

Build: draw.py (Pillow, build-time only) renders a 1024x1024 warm cream
letter with large readable serif text plus simple Pillow-shape illustrations
(small house, garden, steaming cup, comfy chair by the window, cat).
main.py is stdlib-only and copies the rendered entry.png byte-for-byte to
the output directory. README.md, MIT LICENSE, .makefun/owner.json,
CANDIDATES.md (5 design concepts) included.
